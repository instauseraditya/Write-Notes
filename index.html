About Write Notes

Write Notes is a productivity Chrome extension designed to keep your thoughts next to the content you’re already viewing. Instead of switching to a separate notes app, you open a floating note on the page: write rich text, sketch on a canvas, or build a small map of connected ideas.

Notes are stored locally in your browser using Chrome’s storage APIs. We do not require an account to try the product. Optional Write Notes Pro is a yearly digital license delivered as license keys after a successful payment through our payment processor.

This website is the official product page for the extension and is intended for the Chrome Web Store listing, user information, and legal notices (privacy, terms, and refund policy).

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you install or buy.

Is the 7-day trial really free? Do I need a card?
Yes. The trial is free and does not require a credit or debit card. You only pay if you choose to buy Write Notes Pro after (or during) the trial.
What happens when the trial ends?
Pro features such as Draw, Nodes, and Export may lock until you activate a Pro license key. You can purchase Pro from the extension popup and paste a key to unlock again.
Is Write Notes Pro a yearly plan?
Pro is sold as a yearly plan. You pay for one year of Pro access and receive three license keys for up to three devices. Renewal and billing details appear at checkout with our payment partner.
How many devices can I use with one purchase?
One Pro purchase includes three license keys. Each key works on one device at a time, so you can run Pro on up to three devices. Deactivate a device in the popup if you need to move a key.
Can I get a refund after buying Pro?
Pro is a digital yearly plan delivered as keys. Payments are non-refundable after success, except where required by law or by the payment provider’s mandatory rules. If keys never arrived due to a confirmed error, contact support.
Where are my notes stored?
Locally in your browser via Chrome storage. We do not operate a note-sync cloud for your content in the core product. Export a zip or PDF if you need a backup or want to move data.
Does it work on every website?
Notes can float on most normal web pages. Restricted pages (for example some chrome:// pages) cannot host content scripts; use Open notes board from the popup instead.
I reinstalled Chrome and lost Pro
Pro is tied to license keys, not only local files. Activate one of your three keys again on the new setup. If a key is still bound to an old device, use Deactivate on the old device if you still have access, or contact support with your purchase email.
Who processes the payment?
Checkout is handled by our payment partner (Dodo Payments). Card data is entered on their secure pages. We use payment success information to issue license keys.
Can I share a license key with a friend?
Each key is meant for your own devices under the three-key limit. Public sharing or resale can lead to revocation. Buying a separate Pro license is the correct path for another person.
Is Write Notes affiliated with Google?
No. Write Notes is an independent extension. Chrome and the Chrome Web Store are trademarks of Google LLC. Distribution follows Chrome Web Store policies.
How do I contact support?
Email the support address listed on this page and on the Chrome Web Store listing. Include your purchase email and approximate payment time for license issues.
